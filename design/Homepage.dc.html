Going green

Solvent-free adhesive, recyclable strapping, thinner film where the load allows

Sustainability here is a manufacturing decision, not a label. Our adhesive is water-based, so there is no solvent recovery to manage. Our PP strapping replaces steel on most pallet loads and goes back into the recycling stream. Where a carton is light, we will recommend 38 micron instead of selling you 50.
