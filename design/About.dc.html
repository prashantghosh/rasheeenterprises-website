About Rashee Enterprises

Wherever there are boxes, there is Rashee

We manufacture self-adhesive tapes and polypropylene strapping in Mumbai, and we sell them to anyone who needs them correctly specified — a two-person studio shipping forty parcels a month, and a distributor moving pallets. The company was built on a straightforward observation: most packaging is bought on price per roll, and most packaging failures are caused by that.

Founder's philosophy

We would rather lose a sale than sell the wrong micron

The packaging trade runs on a small dishonesty: a buyer asks for a price, gets quoted a grade, and never learns whether that grade was chosen for their carton or for the supplier's margin. Fifty micron sells better than thirty-eight. It also costs the buyer more and, on a light parcel, holds no better.

So we built the company the other way round. Tell us the carton weight and how it travels, and we will tell you what will hold it — including when that is cheaper than what you asked for. If a competitor's grade suits you better, we will say that too. It costs us the occasional order and it is the reason our accounts stay.
