What one box actually holds
1 box 36–72 rolls
A box of 48mm tape holds 36 rolls; at 24mm it holds 72. At 65m a roll, one box seals roughly 2,300 medium cartons — enough for a season of e-commerce dispatch, all of it printed with your logo.

Why the minimum matters

A minimum is a business decision, not a technical limit

Mounting a printing cylinder takes the same setup whether you order one box or fifty. Most suppliers price that setup by refusing small orders. We price it into the quote instead, which is why the number below is one.

Industry norm 10–25 boxes Roughly 360 to 900 rolls before a single one is used. For a growing brand that is a year of stock bought on a guess about next season's volume, and a warehouse corner that stops being available.
Rashee minimum 1 box Thirty-six rolls, printed to your artwork on the same film and the same adhesive as our largest accounts. One bag — five boxes — for strapping patty. Reorder when you need more; the cylinder stays on file.
